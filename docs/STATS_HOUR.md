# Your hour card spec (Stats card 6)

**Question:** when do I listen?

Deliberately not a clock dial. Your day is drawn as a ribbon, midnight to midnight, and the hour itself is the headline.

- **Sentence above:** "Mostly evenings, peaking at 9 PM." (top part of the day when it holds 35% or more, otherwise "You listen all through the day, peaking at 9 PM."). Respects the 12 or 24 hour setting.
- **Headline numeral:** the selected hour, large, lit like the hero ("9 PM", or "21:00"). Eyebrow reads YOUR PEAK HOUR, or AT THIS HOUR once you touch the ribbon. To its right: the hour's share of all listening, and its time.
- **Ribbon:** a smooth curve through the 24 hours (horizontal tangents at each point, so it never overshoots the data) on a dark band that is a touch lighter at midday, a daylight cue using luminance only, no new colour. Yellow stroke with a fading fill. A hairline, halo and lit dot mark the selected hour. Tap or drag to read any hour, with a light tick when the hour changes. Axis: 12 AM, 6 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM.
- **The day in four parts:** one proportional bar (Night, Morning, Afternoon, Evening), the largest lit yellow, with the four shares in a row under it.
- **Data gate:** hidden until there are at least 10 plays over 3 days (patterns would mislead).
- **Motion:** the curve draws left to right over 1s, once per load, then the dot appears. Reduced motion jumps to final.
- **TalkBack:** one summary with the peak and the four shares.
- **Doorway (later):** hour by weekday heatmap.
